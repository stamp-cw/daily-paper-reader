## 摘要
可靠地检测本地地震并准确识别P波初至是地震学中的基本任务，然而许多现有方法需要大量参数调整或大规模训练数据集。本研究探讨了本福特定律（一种控制自然数据中首位数字出现规律的对数分布）作为本地地震检测统计框架的适用性。我们分析了印度次大陆两个构造环境截然不同的连续地震波形数据：板内德干火山省和活动变形的喜马拉雅地区。通过滑动窗口方法，我们量化了地震振幅首位数字分布随时间对本福特定律的符合程度，并应用自适应归一化方案以考虑台站特定噪声。结果表明，本地地震波形在地震能量起始阶段始终符合本福特定律，而事件前噪声则不符合。基于本福特定律的统计异常与理论P波到达时间高度吻合，检测性能主要受窗口长度控制。这些结果确立了本福特定律作为一种计算成本低、参数少且无需训练的地震检测工具的有效性。

## Abstract
Reliable detection of local earthquakes and accurate identification of P-wave onsets are fundamental tasks in seismology, yet many existing methods to accomplish them require extensive parameter tuning or large training datasets. In this study, we investigate the applicability of Benford's Law - a logarithmic distribution governing the occurrence of leading digits in naturally occurring data - as a statistical framework for local earthquake detection. We analyze continuous seismic waveform data from two contrasting tectonic environments in the Indian subcontinent: the intraplate Deccan Volcanic Province and the actively deforming Himalayan region. Using a sliding-window approach, we quantify the temporal conformity of first-digit distributions of seismic amplitudes to Benford's Law and apply an adaptive normalization scheme to account for station-specific noise. Our results show that local earthquake waveforms consistently conform to Benford's Law during the onset of seismic energy, while pre-event noise does not. Benford-derived statistical anomalies align closely with theoretical P-wave arrival times, with detection performance primarily controlled by window length. These results establish Benford's Law as a computationally inexpensive, parameter-light, and training-free tool for local earthquake detection.
